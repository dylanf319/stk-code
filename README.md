# SuperTuxKart Modern Extension - by dylanf319

SuperTuxKart Modern Extension (STKME) is a SuperTuxKart Mod by dylanf319 that increases the difficulty more difficult than SuperTux and with extra modifications

<img width="2560" height="1369" alt="post 1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1d1ba15f-74d4-4573-bbd7-9f80e0da437b" />

Content:
- Difficulty: 
   - The new difficulty level (and no other option is available) will be "HardMode"
   - The Bots/AI karts will have the ability to have the acceleration, speed, weight, and Nitro efficiency at maximum with a kart type of their own and exclusive to the Bots/AI and that surpasses any of the 3 standard types of STK (Light, Medium, Heavy).
   - Also in the games, the game randomly selects some Bot/AI karts to have the ability of double the efficiency of the exclusive kart type of Bots/AI (to identify which ones, the appearance of the Bot/AI will flash in red during the game).
   - The player can only use one of the 3 standard types of karts and not the exclusive type for Bots/AI.
- Music: Some music from the original game will be replaced and remixes of STK music (if any), music from other games, or music from anywhere will be added.
- Karts: Old and discarded karts from versions 0.7 - 0.8.1 (and some from 0.6 ported from add-ons) will return to STKME to replace some current karts:
   - Returning go-karts:
     - Tux: 0.8.1
     - Gnu: 0.8.1
     - Evil Tux: 0.7 (unused)
     - Konqi: 0.9.2
     - Beastie: 1.3
     - Hexley: 0.9.2
     - Adiumy: 0.8.1
     - Gooey: 0.7.2
     - Mr. Iceblock: 0.7 (unused)
     - Mozilla: 0.8
     - Wilber: 0.9.2
   - Karts that will not be eliminated:
     - Emule
     - Gavroche
     - Nolok
     - Pidgin
     - Puffy
     - Suzanne
     - Xue
- New PowerUps: completely new and useless for defeating karts in various ways to win or lose.
   - Items:
     - IceBlock: It's the same as the bowling ball but with a slightly faster speed and can crash into several walls without exploding, and can only shoot at one kart at a time.
     - SmartBlock: similar to a cupcake but the behavior is almost the same as the IceBlock but pink in color, when launched it launches at extreme speed at the start, it has the ability to crash into a row of karts (maximum 10 - 15 karts in a row).
     - Mirror: a bubble shield will be created that will last 5 seconds; if a project collides, it will be turned around.
     - Toxic Gas: It will give you an infected nitro bottle and when you use it, the outlet tube will release toxic gas, causing the karts to lower their top speed and also impairing the karts' vision.
   - Bananas:
     - Nuclear Bomb: It looks just like a time bomb, but you won't have much time to get rid of it, and when you explode, it will also gently explode other karts if they are nearby. Also, when you explode, you will fly higher, losing a little more time to get back to the track.
- Server Online:
   - Any Server:
     - They will not be able to connect due to the incompatibility of the Mod with the original game.
   - Exclusive mod server:
     - You will be able to play with the new PowerUps and Karts.
     - No player will have the skill of the Bots/AI because pro players could beat them against a player with high ping or very low FPS.
     - And of course, it couldn't be missing with the Addons.

That's all, for now you can only play with the karts using a simple installation file (consider it a DEMO version)
